Parkinator 3000: IoT Optical Parking System

Role: Hardware & Test Automation Lead

The Challenge: Designing a reliable mechatronics optical sensor rig capable of accurately monitoring campus parking availability for 30,000+ students under dynamic, high-traffic boundary conditions.

My Process & Technical Contribution: I led the test fixture design and development, bridging hardware and software. I utilized Python to author automated testing scripts that calibrated, logged, and tested BH1750 optical light sensors during lab measurement. I then completed extensive data analysis to characterize sensor states and evaluate system-level impacts.

The Result: The system achieved 95% optical detection accuracy across simulated high-traffic boundary conditions. By summarizing the results and validating the technical viability, the prototype was successfully presented at the IDE Design Expo.

Pneumatic Wobbler Engine

Role: Mechanical Design & Manufacturing Lead

The Challenge: Designing and manufacturing a near-frictionless pneumatic engine that prevents crankshaft deflection and air-seal leakage under dynamic boundary conditions.

My Process & Technical Contribution: I used SOLIDWORKS to design and detail 10+ custom components with ASME Y14.5 GD&T drawing packages. As the manufacturing lead, I machined the parts on a manual lathe and mill. I also optimized the pivot-spring preload and performed dial-indicator deflection measurement across the stationary frame to ensure tight tolerances.

The Result: The design achieved a 100% first-pass assembly fit, which I verified using mechanical instrumentation and tools including micrometers, calipers, and dial indicators. The engine successfully sustained low-friction operation at under 6 PSI during competitive lab testing.
